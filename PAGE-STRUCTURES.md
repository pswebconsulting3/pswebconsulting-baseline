# Page structures per site type

Default sitemaps and section orders. Proposed at kickoff and edited per client — a starting point, not a template to copy blindly. Every page has one job; it's written next to the page name.

Global on every site: header · footer · 404 · privacy policy · cookie policy · terms (where selling) · cookie banner.

---

## 1. Brochure / informational

**Sitemap:** Home · About · Services (or What we do) · Single service × n · Contact
**Optional:** Team · Gallery · Blog · FAQ

**Home — job: explain who they are and get the visitor to contact**
1. Hero — split or full-bleed image
2. Logo strip or one-line credibility (if real)
3. Intro / what we do — split 60/40
4. Services — 3-up cards
5. Why us / approach — split, or stats
6. Testimonial — large single quote
7. CTA band
8. Footer

**About — job: build trust in the people**
Hero minimal · story (split) · values or approach · team (3- or 4-up) · testimonial · CTA

**Contact — job: make contacting effortless**
Hero minimal · split: form (left) + details, hours, map (right) · FAQ (short)

## 2. Service business / lead generation

**Sitemap:** Home · Services · Single service × n · About · Reviews / Case studies · Contact / Get a quote
**Optional:** Areas served · Pricing · Blog · FAQ

**Home — job: one enquiry (call, form or quote)**
1. Hero — split: H1 states the service + area, primary CTA (quote/call), secondary (services); trust line under CTAs (rating, years, accreditation)
2. Logo strip / accreditations
3. Services — 3-up or 4-up cards linking to service pages
4. How it works — 3–4 steps (a genuine sequence, numbers allowed)
5. Results — stats or case study cards
6. Testimonials — 3-up or carousel-free grid
7. FAQ — top 5 objections
8. CTA band — repeat the primary action
9. Footer with phone number

**Single service — job: convert visitors searching for this service**
Hero split (service H1 + CTA) · problem/outcome · what's included · process · case study / testimonial · pricing or "from" price (if allowed) · FAQ (service-specific, with schema) · CTA · related services

**Quote / contact — job: complete the form**
Minimal hero · short form (≤ 6 fields) · what happens next (3 steps) · trust signals beside the form

Rules: phone number clickable in the header on mobile. Primary CTA visible above the fold on desktop and mobile. Same CTA wording everywhere.

## 3. WooCommerce shop

**Sitemap:** Home · Shop (all products) · Category × n · Single product · Cart · Checkout · My account · About · Contact · Shipping & returns · FAQ
**Optional:** Blog / journal · Lookbook · Gift cards · Wholesale

**Home — job: get visitors into a category or best-seller**
1. Hero — full-bleed image or split, one CTA to shop
2. Category tiles — 3- or 4-up, image-led
3. Best-sellers / new in — 4-up product cards
4. Brand story — split
5. USP strip — free shipping, returns, secure payment (only real ones)
6. Reviews
7. Second product row or featured collection
8. Newsletter signup
9. Footer with payment icons

**Category / shop — job: find the product**
Minimal hero (category name + short description) · filter bar (category, price, attributes) + sort · 4-up product grid (2-up on mobile) · pagination or load more · category SEO text below grid

**Single product — job: add to cart**
Breadcrumbs · gallery (left) + summary (right: title, price, rating, short description, variations, qty, add to cart, delivery info, trust) · tabs or accordion (description, specs, shipping, reviews) · related products 4-up

**Cart — job: proceed to checkout**
Line items + sticky summary · free-shipping progress (if offered) · continue shopping link

**Checkout — job: pay**
No header nav and no footer clutter · express payment first · single column on mobile · order summary collapsible on mobile · guest checkout allowed

## 4. Booking / appointments

**Sitemap:** Home · Services / Treatments · Single service × n · Book now · About / Team · Prices · Contact · FAQ
**Optional:** Gift vouchers · Reviews · Blog

**Home — job: start a booking**
1. Hero — split, primary CTA "Book now", secondary "View treatments"
2. Services — 3-up cards with duration and from-price
3. How booking works — 3 steps
4. Team — 3- or 4-up
5. Reviews
6. Location + opening hours — split with map
7. FAQ (cancellations, parking, what to bring)
8. CTA band — Book now
9. Footer with hours and address

**Single service — job: book this service**
Hero split · what it is / who it's for · duration, price, preparation · results / gallery · FAQ · sticky "Book this" CTA on mobile

**Book now — job: complete the booking**
Minimal hero · booking widget (Amelia / Bookly / Calendly / external) styled to tokens in a narrow container · what happens next · cancellation policy summary

Rules: "Book now" in the header on every page. Booking reachable in ≤ 2 taps from any page on mobile.

**Case study:** hero image 16:9 · summary (client, scope, year) · challenge · approach · results (stats) · image sequence · next project

## Section order rules (all types)

- The hero says what the business does and for whom, with one primary action.
- Proof (logos, stats, reviews) appears within the first three sections.
- Never two sections with the same layout back to back.
- Alternate backgrounds no more than one step (bg → surface → bg); one inverse band per page, usually the CTA.
- The page ends with the primary action repeated.
